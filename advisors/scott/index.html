About Scott

Scott Cook is a commercial real estate advisor serving investors, owners, and developers in Hamilton County, the greater Indianapolis market, and across Indiana. He specializes in multi-family, industrial, and land, and brings 31 years of experience owning, managing, and brokering investment properties.

That background lets him view a deal the way an owner does, weighing operations, financing, and long-term value alongside price. Scott is committed to mentoring newer agents and to giving clients honest, well-researched advice.

Scott's career began in the mid-1990s and has spanned the full real estate life cycle. As an asset manager, he has optimized portfolios for institutional and private clients. He later owned and operated a property management company, then built and led a commercial brokerage franchise under one of the world's most recognized real estate brands, where he managed a team of professionals and guided clients through complex transactions.

Scott holds the Certified Property Manager (CPM) designation, one of the industry's most respected credentials in property and asset management. It gives him a practical understanding of what drives net operating income and, in turn, what a property is really worth. He represents both buyers and sellers in acquisitions, dispositions, and 1031 exchanges, and also advises on the sale of operating businesses.

Whether you are selling a multi-family property, looking for industrial space, or evaluating a land opportunity, he will help you make a confident decision.

The question

"What is it worth?" is always the first question.

The answer is not a number, it is a buyer. Who would actually pay for this, and what has to be true before they will?

Who answers it

Red Ball Commercial Group answers that.

Office, industrial, land, retail, mixed use, in Ohio, Indiana, Illinois and California. Each state is worked by an advisor licensed there.
